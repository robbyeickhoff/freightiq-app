# Field Note

Title: Founding Driver rewards program for first 10 users

Summary:
Consider launching a small, manually managed rewards program for FreightIQ’s first 10 legitimate active users.

Recommended concept:
Create an invitation-only **FreightIQ Founding 10** program rather than building a full rewards system into the app.

Potential benefits:
- Encourage early participation and quality Stop Intel contributions
- Create a small real-world driver advisory group
- Reward the users who help shape the product
- Generate stronger early loyalty and word-of-mouth
- Identify confusing features, missing information, and regional differences

Possible rewards:
- Free lifetime access to future paid driver features
- Permanent Founding Driver badge or recognition
- Early access to new features
- Direct input into product decisions
- Optional practical reward such as a FreightIQ hat or modest gift card

Qualification should be based on useful participation, not simply registration.

Example requirements:
- Complete a driver profile
- Submit 5–10 legitimate, useful Stop Intel reports
- Provide one round of honest product feedback
- Remain active during a defined early testing period

Important guardrails:
- Cap the program firmly at 10 users
- Track participation manually at first
- Do not build rewards infrastructure into the app yet
- Review submissions for quality before issuing rewards
- Avoid leading with cash, which could encourage low-quality reports

Priority:
Early-user growth and validation idea — worth exploring, but should remain lightweight

Goal:
Recruit and retain a small group of committed founding drivers who improve FreightIQ through genuine use, quality contributions, and practical feedback.
