# FreightIQ Field Notes Inbox

## Purpose

This file is the permanent running inbox for ideas captured through **FreightIQ Field Notes — Capture Mode**.

Entries remain here until they are processed through **FreightIQ Field Notes — End-of-Day Review** and marked Solidified, Ready for Workflow, Deferred, Combined, or Discarded.

This inbox is not automatically a product backlog, task list, or documentation queue.
