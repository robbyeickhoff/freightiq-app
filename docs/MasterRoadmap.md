# FreightIQ Master Roadmap

## Purpose

The Master Roadmap is the navigation system for FreightIQ's long-term development.

It does not contain feature backlogs or active tasks.

Instead, it organizes the guiding philosophies that shape every product, engineering, and business decision.

Each roadmap document answers a different question about how FreightIQ should evolve. Together, they form the operating system that guides the project.

## How to Use the Roadmap

The Roadmap is designed to guide decisions, not manage day-to-day work.

When working on FreightIQ:

1. Start with the Product Vision to understand why FreightIQ exists and what it is ultimately trying to become.

2. Use the Master Roadmap to understand how major product decisions should be approached.

3. Reference the individual Roadmap documents whenever making decisions about product design, engineering, product refinement, driver validation, or documentation.

4. Use the Master TODO to determine what should be worked on next.

5. Use the Engineering Playbook and Release Process to execute that work safely, consistently, and professionally.

The documents are designed to work together. Each has a distinct purpose, and together they create a repeatable system for making high-quality decisions.
