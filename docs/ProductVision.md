# FreightIQ Product Vision

## Purpose

FreightIQ is a system for preserving operational knowledge.

Maps are not the product.

Knowledge is the product.

The map is the delivery mechanism.

## Design Principles

- Driver-first
- Fast
- Simple
- Trustworthy
- Professional
- Apple-quality polish

## Foundational Philosophy

The Product Vision defines why FreightIQ exists.

The Master Roadmap preserves the enduring philosophies that guide how FreightIQ evolves.

Together, they ensure that every product, engineering, and business decision supports FreightIQ's long-term vision while remaining consistent as the project grows.
