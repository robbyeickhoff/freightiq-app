# Founding Driver Program Website — V1 Concept

## Status

Future website concept captured on 2026-08-05 for later design and build planning.

This document records the intended public recruitment page and private Founder experience. It does not authorize website implementation, deployment, authentication, database, or release changes. It also does not change the approved Founding Driver Program V0 scope or the currently paused Phase 3 mobile work.

The private Founder experience may inform Phase 5 of `FoundingDriverProgramV0.md`. Public recruitment remains a future expansion outside V0 unless separately reviewed and approved.

---

## Core Design Principle

Every page should reinforce that Founding Drivers are **building the map and preserving professional knowledge**, not simply collecting rewards.

The program should make Founders feel like builders of FreightIQ—not participants in a generic incentive program.

---

## Public Page — Recruitment

### Goal

Inspire the right drivers to become FreightIQ Founding Drivers.

### Proposed Sections

- Hero: **Become a FreightIQ Founding Driver**
- Why FreightIQ Exists
  - Preserve real driver knowledge.
  - Help drivers approach unfamiliar stops with confidence.
- Why Become a Founder
  - Shape FreightIQ.
  - Receive early access to features.
  - Earn exclusive Founder recognition.
  - Help future drivers.
- How It Works
  - Join → Contribute → Earn → Unlock
- Current Rewards
- Frequently Asked Questions
- Apply / Join call to action

### Primary Messaging Theme

> You're not just earning rewards—you're helping build the future of freight navigation.

### Experience Guardrails

- Driver-to-driver, practical, and confident.
- Focus on trusted professional contributors, not mass signups.
- Explain the purpose before emphasizing rewards.
- Avoid corporate recruiting language, hype, or social-media framing.
- Make the commitment and expectations easy to understand.

---

## Founder Dashboard — Members Only

### Goal

Make Founders feel like builders, not just reward participants.

### Proposed Sections

- Founder welcome and badge
- Progress bar
- Next milestone
- Recent activity
- Friendly leaderboard
- Milestones and achievements
- Your Impact
  - Drivers helped
  - Intel views
  - Stops improved
  - Reports submitted
- Founder news and upcoming features

### Experience Priorities

- Personal progress remains primary.
- Community recognition remains secondary and professional.
- Impact should connect contributions to real driver value.
- Rewards should be visible without becoming the page's entire purpose.
- Any metric shown must be supportable by trustworthy FreightIQ data.

## Visual Direction

Use the approved FreightIQ **Sunrise System — Concept D** as the visual reference:

- Charcoal and near-black foundation
- Sunrise gradient for progress, milestones, and positive emphasis
- Copper accents
- Slate supporting surfaces
- Stone text and neutral contrast
- FreightIQ Sunrise icon and wordmark
- Mountain, road-ahead, and first-light imagery where appropriate

The Founding Driver pages should feel like part of FreightIQ, not a separate sub-brand. The treatment should be motivating, polished, and professional without becoming childish, noisy, or game-like.

---

## Relationship to the Approved Program Build

- The private dashboard concept expands the existing V0 personal progress and leaderboard direction.
- The public recruitment page is intentionally outside the current V0 build specification.
- “Your Impact” metrics and Recent Activity require later verification against available, privacy-safe data.
- Operations Board access remains a separate future feature dependency.
- No implementation should begin from this concept alone; the applicable FreightIQ approval and build workflow must be started first.
