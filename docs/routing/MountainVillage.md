# Mountain Village

## Purpose

Document confirmed routing knowledge for the Mountain Village delivery zone, with the current rules focused on the Mountain Village Boulevard core.

Use this file to preserve operational flow and stop-specific access knowledge. Do not treat today-specific stop order as permanent unless the underlying routing reason is documented here.

---

## Zone Characteristics

- The Mountain Village Boulevard core is a dead-end delivery area with one primary way in and out.
- The route enters the core from the west and returns the same general way.
- Internal stop order should preserve direction of travel and minimize unnecessary crossings, turnarounds, and backtracking.
- Broader Mountain Village-area subzones, including Ophir, Prospect Creek, Adams Ranch, and other areas, require their own confirmed internal rules.

---

## Default Internal Flow

When entering the Mountain Village Boulevard core:

1. Travel west to east.
2. Work active stops on the south side of Mountain Village Boulevard while continuing eastbound.
3. Continue to the easternmost active stop.
4. Turn around.
5. Work active stops on the north side while traveling westbound toward the exit.

This is the default flow, not an absolute rule.

A confirmed safety, access, backing, appointment, or freight constraint may justify a local exception.

---

## Physical Location Over Consignee Name

Route the physical delivery location, not only the customer, contractor, or consignee name shown on the manifest.

A contractor or interior designer may be receiving freight at a larger property, hotel, residence, or jobsite.

### Confirmed Example

- **457 Mountain Village Boulevard** is **Mountain Lodge**, a large hotel.
- A manifest may show another business or contractor name, such as an interior designer working inside one of the units.
- For routing purposes, classify and reason about the stop as **Mountain Lodge**.

Do not create permanent routing rules around a temporary consignee name.

---

## Confirmed Operational Exception: Mountain Lodge

### Default Geographic Position

Mountain Lodge is on the north side of Mountain Village Boulevard.

Under the default Mountain Village flow, a north-side stop would normally be completed westbound after reaching the easternmost active stop and turning around.

### Backing Constraint

The Mountain Lodge Delivery Zone requires a tricky back-in.

The back-in is safer and easier when the truck approaches while facing east because this avoids a blind-side backing maneuver.

### Routing Rule

Mountain Lodge may be serviced eastbound before the normal north-side return sequence when doing so provides the safer sight-side backing approach.

This is a documented operational exception to the default side-of-road flow.

The reason for the exception is:

- safer backing
- better visibility
- easier truck positioning

The rule is **not** that Mountain Lodge is always second, always early, or always serviced before every other stop.

Its placement still depends on:

- today's active stops
- current direction of travel
- appointments
- access conditions
- other confirmed operational constraints

## Routing Priority

Within Mountain Village, use this order of judgment:

1. Safety and backing visibility
2. Truck access and maneuverability
3. Confirmed delivery-zone approach
4. Directional and side-of-road flow
5. Avoiding backtrcking
6. Distance and convenience

A local exception should remain local. Do not disrupt the full Mountain Village flow more than necessary.
