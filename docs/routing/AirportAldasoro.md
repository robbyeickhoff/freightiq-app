## Zone Characteristics

Airport / Aldasoro follows Downtown Telluride and normally represents the final Telluride-area delivery zone before continuing toward the West End or returning east.

The zone consists primarily of residential mountain roads branching from Airport Road and Aldasoro Boulevard.

Unlike Downtown Telluride, the road network is less grid-oriented and more residential.

Unlike Mountain Village, the zone generally has fewer dead ends and operational constraints, although individual roads may still require special consideration.

---

## Operational Philosophy

Airport / Aldasoro should normally be completed during one continuous visit.

The objective is to preserve steady progression through the residential road network while positioning the truck for the next Macro Zone.

Appointments, freight accessibility, trailer loading, weather, or documented operational exceptions may justify local changes.

Local adjustments should remain local whenever practical.

---

## Macro Position

Normal previous zone:

- Downtown Telluride

Normal next zone:

- Norwood
- Gateway
- Grand Junction, when no West End work exists

Default Macro Flow:

```text
Mountain Village
→ Downtown Telluride
→ Airport / Aldasoro
→ West End or Return
```

---

## Entry

Operational entry begins as the truck leaves Downtown Telluride and enters Airport Road.

Airport Road, County Road T60, forms the primary entrance into the Airport / Aldasoro zone.

---

## Exit

Operational exit occurs after completing Airport / Aldasoro and continuing toward the next Macro Zone.

Normally this means:

- Norwood
- West End
- or returning east toward Ridgway and Grand Junction, depending on the day's route.

## Transition Traps

### Leaving completed residential areas

Avoid leaving a completed neighborhood only to return later unless required by a significant operational constraint.
