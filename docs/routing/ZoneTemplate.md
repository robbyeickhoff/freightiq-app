# FreightIQ Zone Template

## Purpose

Use this template when creating a new operational Zone document.

A Zone document explains:

- What belongs in the zone
- Where the zone sits in the larger route
- How drivers normally enter, work, and leave it
- Which rules are dependable versus merely preferred

Do not use a Zone document for stop-specific details that belong in customer intel.

Customer-specific examples may appear only when they establish or illustrate a durable zone-level routing rule. Detailed operating instructions still belong in customer intel.

Zone membership is a durable classification. Service timing may change according to the route.

## Boundary and Transition Logic

Assign each stop an authoritative home zone based on its physical road position and normal routing behavior.

Do not reclassify a stop solely to fit today’s route.

Some stops lie near the operational edge of a zone. For those stops, exact service timing may depend on the next active zone and the direction of travel.

For each edge-of-zone road, area, or stop, document:

- Authoritative home zone
- Relevant edge or Micro Zone
- Normal approach direction
- Natural onward transition
- Conditions that create backtracking
- Valid alternate service direction

When an edge stop can be serviced from more than one direction, choose the option that best preserves the full route’s forward flow.
