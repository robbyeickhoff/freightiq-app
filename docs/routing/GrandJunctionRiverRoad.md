# Grand Junction — River Road

## Purpose

Document confirmed operational routing knowledge for the River Road parent
zone in the Grand Junction Valley.

River Road is a dense, independent delivery territory. A Grand Junction
trailer is normally assigned to one parent zone, so River Road is not one step
in a daily sequence through the other Grand Junction zones.
